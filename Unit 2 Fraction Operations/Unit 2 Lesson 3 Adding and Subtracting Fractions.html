Rules to remember
  • Only matching pieces can be added. 3 eighths and 2 eighths are 5 eighths, the same way 3 apples and 2 apples are 5 apples. Add the tops and keep the bottom: 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8. Never add the bottoms.
  • Different bottoms? Rebuild them first. Use the lowest common multiple of the two bottoms, then multiply each top by whatever its own bottom was multiplied by. For 1/4 + 1/6, the LCM of 4 and 6 is 12, so 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12.
  • Mixed numbers become improper fractions before you calculate. 2 and 1/3 = (2×3 + 1)/3 = 7/3. Then add or subtract as usual. Do not add the whole parts and the fraction parts in separate piles.
  • Subtraction needs the same setup as addition. Same bottom first, then subtract the tops: 5/6 − 1/6 = 4/6 = 2/3.
  • Every final answer is a fraction in lowest terms, not a mixed number. Check at the end whether the top and bottom still share a factor.
