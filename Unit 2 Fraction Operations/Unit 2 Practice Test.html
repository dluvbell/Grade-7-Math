Rules to remember
  • This is a test, not practice. A wrong answer is final for that question — you cannot pick again. The next question will be the same type.
  • Earning your way past. Each wrong answer adds one to the number of correct answers you need. One wrong means two correct; two wrong means three.
  • Three wrong in a row stops the test and sends you back to the exact lesson and type you need to practise.
  • Nothing tells you which type a question is. Working that out is the test. The hint names the ideas the question is built from, so open it only when stuck.
  • Every answer is a fraction in lowest terms, never a mixed number, and every fraction here is positive. Change mixed numbers to improper fractions before calculating.
  • Application means you know the steps but the question runs backwards or joins two ideas. Thinking means the question does not tell you which method to use — deciding that is the first step of the solution.
