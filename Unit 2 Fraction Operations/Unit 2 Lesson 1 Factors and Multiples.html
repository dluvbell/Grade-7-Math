Unit 2 Lesson 1: Factors and Multiples

Find factors, multiples, the greatest common factor and the lowest common multiple - and decide which one a problem needs.

Rules to remember
  • A factor divides evenly. The factors of 12 are {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12}. Every factor is at most the number itself, and 1 is a factor of everything.
  • A multiple is the number times 1, 2, 3, ... The multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, 16, ... Every multiple is at least the number itself, and the list never ends.
  • Greatest common factor (GCF). List the factors of each number, keep the ones on every list, take the biggest. It is never larger than the smallest number you started with.
  • Lowest common multiple (LCM). Count up in each number and take the first value they share. It is never smaller than the largest number you started with.
  • Which one does a word problem need? Splitting into equal groups → GCF. Two things repeating and meeting again → LCM. Ask whether the answer should come out smaller than what you started with, or bigger.
  • Why this lesson comes first. The GCF is how you write a fraction in lowest terms, and the LCM is how you give two fractions the same denominator before adding them.
