Rules to remember
  • Equivalent fractions. Multiply or divide the top and the bottom by the same number. 3/4 = (3×5)/(4×5) = 15/20. Adding the same number to the top and bottom does not work: 3/4 is not 8/9.
  • Lowest terms. Divide the top and the bottom by their greatest common factor. 18/24 has GCF 6, so 18/24 = 3/4. If you can still divide both by something bigger than 1, you are not finished.
  • Common denominator. Use the lowest common multiple of the two bottoms. For 5/6 and 3/8, the LCM of 6 and 8 is 24, so 5/6 = 20/24 and 3/8 = 9/24. Whatever you multiply a bottom by, multiply that top by the same.
  • Comparing. Same bottom → compare the tops. Different bottoms → give them a common denominator first, then compare the tops.
  • Improper and mixed. 7/3 = 2 and 1/3, because 7 ÷ 3 is 2 with 1 left over. Going back: 2 and 1/3 = (2×3 + 1)/3 = 7/3.
  • How answers are written in this unit. Every final answer is a fraction in lowest terms, not a mixed number.
