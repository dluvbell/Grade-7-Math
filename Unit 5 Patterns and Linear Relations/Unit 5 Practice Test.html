Rules to remember
  • Nothing here tells you which lesson it came from. Reading the question and deciding what it is asking for is part of the test. If you are stuck, the hint says which type it belongs to.
  • Application questions look like the ones you practised, with the numbers mixed up. Thinking questions do not tell you the method — you choose it first, then use it.
  • Term number and term value. The rate is the jump between terms; the initial value is the term value at term number 0, one step before term 1.
  • In y = mx + b, m is the rate and b is the initial value. A falling relation is written y = 40 − 3x in the lessons; in Thinking questions you may also meet it written −3x + 40, which means exactly the same thing.
  • Near or far? A term just past what you were given can be walked to. A term far away needs the equation.
  • Given the value, find the term number by balancing: take the initial value off both sides, then divide both sides by the rate. Always substitute back to check.
  • Get one wrong and the same type comes back. You need one more correct answer than the number of mistakes you have made.
