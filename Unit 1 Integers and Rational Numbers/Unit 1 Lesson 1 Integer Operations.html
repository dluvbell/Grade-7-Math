Rules to remember
  • Adding, same signs. Add the sizes and keep that sign. (-4) + (-3) = (-7).
  • Adding, different signs. Take the smaller size from the bigger size and keep the sign of the bigger one. 7 + (-12) = (-5).
  • Two signs touching join into one. Minus and minus make a plus: 5 - (-3) = 5 + 3. Plus and minus make a minus: 5 + (-3) = 5 - 3. Minus and plus make a minus: 5 - 3 stays as it is. The first number never changes.
  • On a number line, a positive move goes right and a negative move goes left. Right is always larger, left is always smaller.
  • Below is negative, above is positive. Below zero, below sea level, below the surface and overdrawn are all negative. 3 metres below sea level is (-3) metres.
