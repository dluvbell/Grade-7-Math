Rules to remember
  • Negative numbers work backwards. With positive numbers, bigger digits mean a bigger number: 5 > 2. With negative numbers it is the opposite — bigger digits mean a smaller number: (-5) < (-2). So between two negatives, the one closer to zero is the greater one.
  • On a number line, right is always larger and left is always smaller — on both sides of zero.
  • Size of one space = (distance between two labelled numbers) ÷ (number of spaces between them). Count the spaces, not the tick marks — 11 marks make 10 spaces.
  • From Lesson 1: to find a distance you subtract, and two signs touching join into one. (-1.5) - (-2) = (-1.5) + 2 = 0.5.
  • Fraction → decimal: divide the top by the bottom. Decimal → fraction: the place value of the last digit gives the denominator, then divide top and bottom by their greatest common factor.
