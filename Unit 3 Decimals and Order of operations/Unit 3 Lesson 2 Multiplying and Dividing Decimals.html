Rules to remember
  • Multiplying or dividing by 10, 100 or 1000 slides the digits. The digits never change — only the place they sit in. 3.42 × 100 = 342 and 56.7 ÷ 10 = 5.67.
  • Multiplying by a number smaller than 1 makes it smaller; dividing by a number smaller than 1 makes it bigger. 3 ÷ 0.1 = 30, because you are asking how many tenths fit into 3.
  • To multiply decimals, ignore the points, then count them back in. 0.4 × 0.7: work out 4 × 7 = 28, and since the question had two decimal places altogether, so does the answer: 0.28.
  • To divide by a decimal, make the divisor a whole number first by multiplying both numbers by the same power of ten. 7.5 ÷ 0.8 = 75 ÷ 8. Scaling only one of them changes the answer.
  • Read the rounding instruction — it is part of the question. Not every division comes out exactly. "To two decimal places" means the answer shows two, even if the last one is a 0.
  • Estimate first to check where the point goes. If 4.9 × 6.1 is about 30, then an answer of 2.989 or 298.9 has the point in the wrong place.
