Rules to remember
  • The order is: brackets first, then × and ÷, then + and −. So 2 + 3 × 4 = 2 + 12 = 14, not 20.
  • × and ÷ are equal partners, and so are + and −. When two equal partners meet, work left to right: 24 ÷ 6 × 2 = 4 × 2 = 8, and 10 − 3 + 4 = 7 + 4 = 11. This is where most mistakes happen.
  • Brackets are an instruction, not decoration. Everything inside them is finished before anything outside them starts — and inside the brackets the same order applies again.
  • Powers are not part of this lesson. You will meet them in a later unit, and the order will grow to make room for them.
  • About the letters in Type 5. In a property like a × b = b × a, the letters just mean "any number you like" — the rule holds whatever you put in. Everywhere else in this unit, every calculation and every answer is a number.
  • Check your answer against a rough estimate. If 4.8 × 3 + 1.2 is about 15 + 1, an answer of 28.8 means the order went wrong.
