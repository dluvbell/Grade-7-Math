Converting between the three ways of writing the same amount

Rules to remember
  • Percent means hundredths. Per cent is "for each hundred", so 45% is 45 out of 100, or 0.45, or 45/100. Every conversion in this lesson comes back to that one idea.
  • Percent → decimal: divide by 100. The point moves two places left. 45% → 0.45, and 4.5% → 0.045.
  • Decimal → percent: multiply by 100. The point moves two places right. 0.6 → 60%, and 0.423 → 42.3%. A percent may have a decimal in it.
  • Fraction → percent, route 1: make the bottom number 100. For 3/20, 20 × 5 = 100, so multiply top and bottom by 5 to get 15/100 = 15%.
  • Fraction → percent, route 2: divide. When nothing multiplies the bottom number up to 100 — eighths, sixteenths, fortieths — divide the top by the bottom instead: 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625 = 62.5%.
  • Percent → fraction: write it over 100, then simplify. 45% = 45/100 = 9/20. If the percent has a decimal, clear it first: 12.5% = 125/1000 = 1/8.
  • Percents can be greater than 100%. 100% is the whole itself, so 150% is one and a half wholes: 150% = 1.5 = 3/2. Any answer above 100% has to come out bigger than 1.
  • To compare, put everything in one form first. 2/5, 0.35 and 45% cannot be ordered as they stand. As percents they are 40%, 35% and 45%, and the order is clear.
  • Benchmarks worth knowing by heart. 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%, 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%, 1/5 = 0.2 = 20%, 1/8 = 0.125 = 12.5%, 1/10 = 0.1 = 10%.
  • How far the digits go. In this unit a percent is written to at most two decimal places and a decimal to at most four, and every conversion lands exactly. Nothing here needs to be rounded off.
