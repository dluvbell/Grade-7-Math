Finding a percent of an amount in your head

Rules to remember
  • Know these five by heart. 1/2 = 50%, 1/4 = 25%, 1/5 = 20%, 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/10 = 10%. Everything else in this lesson is built out of them.
  • Scale a unit fraction up. If 1/5 = 20%, then 4/5 is four of those: 20% × 4 = 80%. You never have to start a conversion over.
  • 10% moves the digits one place right. 10% of 340 is 34. 10% of 45 is 4.5. No multiplying needed.
  • 1% moves the digits two places right. 1% of 340 is 3.4, and 1% of 45 is 0.45.
  • 50% is half. 25% is half of 50%. 5% is half of 10%. Halving is the other mental tool, and it chains: to get 25%, halve twice.
  • 100% is the amount itself, and 200% is twice the amount. A percent below 100% must give an answer smaller than the amount; above 100%, bigger.
  • Any percent can be built from 1%, 5% and 10%. 15% = 10% + 5%. 13% = 10% + 3 × 1%. 35% = 25% + 10%. Take the biggest easy piece that fits, then keep going with what is left.
  • Build the percent first, then apply it. For 15% of $60: 10% is $6.00, 5% is $3.00, so 15% is $9.00.
  • This lesson is always "a percent of an amount". 15% of $60 is $9.00 — that is the part, not the new total. Increasing or decreasing by a percent comes in the next lesson.
  • Money always gets two decimal places. $9, $9.5 and $9.50 are the same amount, but only $9.50 is written properly.
