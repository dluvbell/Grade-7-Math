Rules to remember
  • An inequality is solved exactly like an equation. Undo the operations in reverse order, doing the same thing to both sides.
  • But the answer is a range, not one number. 3x = 12 has one solution; 3x < 12 has every number below 4. That is why the answer gets drawn on a number line.
  • Open circle for < and >; closed circle for ≤ and ≥. The circle says whether the boundary number is itself a solution.
  • The arrow shows the range keeps going. Which way it points is decided by the sign, not by which side of the page the number is on.
  • The sign never turns round in this unit, because every number you multiply or divide by here is positive. (Dividing by a negative does turn it round — that comes later.)
  • Verify with two numbers: one from inside the range and one from outside. The first should work and the second should fail. If they both do the same thing, something has gone wrong.
