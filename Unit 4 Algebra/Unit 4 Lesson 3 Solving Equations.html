Rules to remember
  • Two methods are shown for every equation. Open the solution and the tabs let you switch between them. Balance Method is the one that keeps working in every later grade; Reverse Flow Chart is the picture the curriculum draws. Learn both, and use whichever you can see faster.
  • An equation is a balance. Whatever you do to one side you must do to the other, or the two sides stop being equal.
  • Undo in reverse order. The last thing done to the letter is the first thing you undo. For 3x + 4 = 25, the +4 goes first, then the ×3.
  • Adding undoes subtracting; multiplying undoes dividing.
  • Collect like terms before you start undoing. 2x + 3x + 4 = 19 has no single chain to reverse until the left side is written as 5x + 4.
  • Always check. Put your answer back into the original equation. If both sides come out the same, you are right and you know it.
